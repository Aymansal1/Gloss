Featurep. 12

Search that reads Japanese

Japanese doesn't put spaces between words, so a plain index misses a name in the middle of a sentence. Gloss indexes overlapping pairs of characters, so it finds it.

Letters

from readers

How do I install it?

Gloss isn't on the Play Store. Download the APK on your phone, open it, and allow your browser to install apps when Android asks. Updates show up inside the app.

Are the translations right?

They're machine-made, so treat them as a good first read rather than gospel. The original scan is always there to check against, and the app says so.

Where do the magazines come from?

From Gaming Alexandria's archive of scans and transcriptions, the same files their desktop app reads. Gloss isn't affiliated with them.

Does it track me?

No accounts, no analytics, no ads. Your library, bookmarks and reading place stay on your phone.

Gloss is free and open source. Scans, transcriptions and translations are Gaming Alexandria's; please support their preservation work. Magazine names and covers belong to their publishers.
